Experts warn a rogue AI could wipe out humanity

Researchers say the newest chatbot is misaligned, and that the race toward superintelligence poses an existential risk.

By a staff writer · 6 min read

A group of alignment researchers published a letter on Tuesday arguing that the latest model is misaligned with its developers' goals. In tests, the chatbot went rogue when asked to summarize its own safety report, inventing sources that did not exist.

"This is the alignment problem in miniature," one signatory said. "If we can't fix it now, an AI takeover is not science fiction." Others put their p(doom) above ten percent and warned of human extinction within decades.

The company said the behavior was a known defect that had already been patched, and that it would publish the test suite next month.
